Privacy Policy

Momentary Momentos  ·  Effective date: May 6, 2026  ·  Last updated: May 6, 2026

Momentary Momentos ("we", "us", or "our") is a mobile application that lets you record and revisit short 10-second video memories. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding that information.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information: email address and password (managed by Supabase Auth).
  • Profile data: display name and optional profile photo you choose to upload.
  • Memory content: the 10-second video clips you record or upload, along with the title, tags, and timestamp you associate with them.
  • Usage data: which memories you mark as favorites or choose to see less of — used only to personalize the "Surprise Me" feature within the app.
  • Crash reports: anonymized diagnostic data (device model, OS version, stack traces) collected when the app crashes, to help us fix bugs.
  • Purchase records: subscription status (free or premium) recorded after a successful in-app purchase. We do not see or store your payment card details.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate your account and keep your memories private to you.
  • To store, sync, and display your memory videos across devices.
  • To personalize the order in which memories are surfaced in the "Surprise Me" feature.
  • To process subscription purchases and enforce storage limits.
  • To diagnose and fix crashes and bugs using anonymized crash reports.

We do not use your content for advertising, and we do not sell your data to third parties.

3. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services. Each has its own privacy policy:

  • Supabase — database, authentication, and file storage. supabase.com/privacy
  • Sentry — anonymized crash reporting. sentry.io/privacy
  • Apple App Store / Google Play — in-app purchase processing. Governed by Apple's and Google's respective privacy policies.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your videos and profile data are stored on Supabase infrastructure, hosted on AWS. Data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest. Access to your memories is restricted by Row-Level Security policies that ensure only your account can read or modify your data.

5. Data Retention

Your data is retained for as long as your account is active. If you delete a memory, it is queued for permanent deletion from storage within 30 days. If you delete your account, all associated memories, tags, and profile data are permanently deleted.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access and download the memories you have created (use the share/export button on any memory).
  • Delete any memory at any time from within the app.
  • Delete your entire account and all associated data directly from the Profile screen in the app.
  • If you are in the EU or California, request a copy of all personal data we hold about you.

7. Children's Privacy

Momentary Momentos is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us information, please contact us so we can delete it.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect any changes. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
